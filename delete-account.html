Delete your Bad Golf account

You can delete your Bad Golf account and its data at any time, either directly in the app or by emailing us. Deletion is permanent.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Bad Golf and sign in.
  2. Go to your Account screen (the same place you signed in).
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and data are removed immediately.

Option 2 — By email

Email Tyler@simplisticfishing.com with the subject “Bad Golf Account Deletion Request” from the email address on your account. We'll process the deletion within 30 days and confirm when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address and sign-in)
  • Your profile (display name) and player link
  • Your saved rounds, scores, statistics and handicap history
  • Your game history and final-standing records
  • Your device push-notification tokens

Note: rounds you played with other people remain visible to those players in their round history (their scorecards include the round you shared), but they are no longer linked to any account of yours.
