# Bad Golf — App Store text, builds 1913 → 1920 (live build: 1912)

## Promotional text
Preview any course before you play: hole-by-hole maps, a flyover and green slopes, now on 10,000+ courses. Plus a heads-up to check the course before your tee time.

## What's New
Course Preview is front and center, and green maps now cover more than 10,000 courses.

COURSE PREVIEW
• At the course? A card on Home shows the course you're at (or a couple nearby) with Preview and Start round right there. It hides once you've played there today.
• Every course page now has big Preview course and Start round buttons.
• Watch a hole-by-hole flyover, see each hole's par and yardage from your tees, and check the greens before you tee off.
• Start round from a preview drops you into setup with the course picked and you already added.
• Preview a course straight from round setup, or from any tee time.
• Not signed in yet? Browse and preview every course before you create an account.

TEE TIMES
• About 1–2 hours before a tee time you're on, Bad Golf sends a reminder. Tap it to preview the course.
• Don't want it? Turn it off in Settings → Notifications → "Course preview before my tee time."
• Opening and accepting a tee time is much faster.

GREEN MAPS
• Green outlines and slope colors now on 10,000+ courses, with new states added across the country.
• Clubs with several courses (like Medinah No. 1, 2 and 3) let you pick the course when you preview.

FIXES
• Faster start-up and event loading.
• Other small fixes and polish.
