# VERIFY_CLIENT: independent check of the client-rooted league defects (build e5a912f, 2026-11-03)

Method:
- Own harness: `verify_client/verify_client.js`. It loads the real app script through `qa/app.js` with my own fake Supabase and inputs, and every expectation is hand-computed in the comments. Output is in `verify_client/verify_client_output.txt`: 15 repros, all reproduced.
- Server: `verify_client/verify_server_probes.sql`. Each probe was one `begin … rollback` block on live, plus read-only SELECTs.
- I did not use the local Postgres copy. Its `league_money_recompute` md5 (8809ec…) does not match live (4a5a74…), and it lacks most `league_*` functions.

Live data (read-only): 5 seasons in total, 3 scramble (team H2H) and 2 stroke_net. Every week is `full18`. None is Stableford, none pays 70/30, none has a `launched_code`, none has GIR or putts pots, no league round starts off 1 or 10, and no member has a non-ASCII name. So **no live season hits any of these today**. Exposure is through options the app offers right now.
