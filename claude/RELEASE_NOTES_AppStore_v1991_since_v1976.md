# Bad Golf — App Store text, builds 1977 → 1991 (live build: 1976)

## Promotional text
The scorecard now reads like the one in your cart: your tee's yardage on every hole, HCP, par and scores. Plus Round Ratings — rate a course and see how your crew rates theirs.

## What's New
A real scorecard, Round Ratings, and a cleaner finish to every round.

SCORECARD
• Every scorecard in the app now reads like the printed card: your tee's yardage on every hole, the HCP row, par, then the scores — on the GPS screen, the scoring screen, the finished-round card, the shared link and the images you text out.
• Phones show the front and back nine stacked so nothing is squeezed; wider screens show all 18 across.
• Playing more than one match? Each one gets its own line, labelled with who it's against.
• The Match / Strokes / Units chips stay put when you swipe to the back nine.
• The GPS scorecard has a ✕ in the corner, and a stray pinch can't push "Back to map" off the screen.

ROUND RATINGS
• After a round, give the course 1–5 stars and a note. Stars show beside every course in the pickers and on the course preview; tap them to see the breakdown and what other players said.
• Not a fan of end-of-round prompts? Turn them off in Settings › Additional settings.

GPS
• Hole yardages for your tee on the GPS readout for every course we've matched to its scorecard, with more courses filling in nightly.

TOURNAMENTS & GAMES
• Closest to the Pin, Skins and Long Putt now settle the moment every card is in — no more waiting on a phone that was left in a pocket.
• Birdie Bump runs live during the round on the GPS screen and the breakdown, so you can see where you stand before the day settles.

FIXES
• Course rows and buttons no longer dodge your tap while the keyboard is up (Android).
• The Firewheel Bridges nine picker can't flip your Champion / Masters choice while it loads, and the start button names the pairing.
• Buffalo Creek and other seed courses show their tee yardage on the website.
• Other small fixes and polish.

## Notes
- Covers v1977–v1991 (main at v1991 as of 2026-11-01, commit 2f8c1cb). The live build is v1976.
- No native (Swift) changes in this range; web deploy and iOS build carry the same app script.
- Promo text is 166 characters (Apple limit 170). What's New is well under 4,000.
- Wording follows the de-gambled rules: units, "settle", no payment terms.
