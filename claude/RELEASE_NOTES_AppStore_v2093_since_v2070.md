# App Store release text — v2093 (last submitted build was v2070)

## What's New
What's new in this update:

• New game: 11's. Keep your best 11 holes and throw out the rest, deciding after every hole. Play it net or gross, as a single game, as Team 11's, or as a pool across a whole tournament.
• A new scorecard on the Score screen. Standings come first, with bigger names and numbers, each player's handicap and net, stroke dots, and skins highlighted. Tap any hole to change a score. The classic scorecard is one tap away.
• The Units tab now names each game behind the numbers and what every player made or lost on it.
• Cross bets between groups have a new, easier screen: tap the players, tap the game, tap the options.
• Quota bets between groups get their own Quota tab, with points shown hole by hole.
• Ties in team games are broken by the next lowest score.
• Guests can find and claim their own spot in a tournament from the invite.
• Putts and greens are only asked for when a game pays on them.
• Finish round appears as soon as the last score goes in, and you can reopen a round straight from the results.
• Faster Home screen and faster joining of a live round.
• Fixes for setting up a tournament on iPhone, the players and handicaps list, and reliability throughout.
