# App Store release text — v2070 (last submitted build was v2066)

## What's New
What's new in this update:

• Scores entered while your phone is out of signal now reach the group as soon as you are back, even when the phone never says so. Retry now works too.
• A bet between players in different groups can be set for 0 units, just for bragging rights.
• The Quota tab lists players 1st to last, with the place in front of each line, so you can see who is winning at a glance.
• A round that starts on the back nine shows holes 10 to 18 first on the scorecard, then the front nine.
• A player who is the only one from his group in a tournament Quota or Stableford pool is scored correctly.
• Adding players 13 and 14 to a tournament opens a new group instead of squeezing them into full ones.
• Plus reliability fixes throughout.
