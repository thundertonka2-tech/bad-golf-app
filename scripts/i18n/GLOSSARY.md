# Bad Golf App — translation glossary (es · ko · ja · zh-Hans)

Locked 2026-10-01. Every translator (human or AI) follows this file.

## Who the reader is
Recreational golfers playing with friends, leagues and charity outings. Friendly, short, phone-sized UI text.

| Lang | Variant | Register |
|---|---|---|
| es | Neutral US/Latin-American Spanish (no vosotros, no Spain-only slang) | informal **tú** |
| ko | Standard Korean | polite-informal **해요체** for sentences; noun phrases for buttons/labels |
| ja | Standard Japanese | concise **です・ます** for sentences; noun/verb-stem for buttons |
| zh | Simplified Chinese (mainland) | concise, **你** (not 您) |

## Format rules
- Keep placeholders `{0}`, `{1}` … exactly. You may reorder them, and you may DROP a placeholder that only carries an English plural "s" (e.g. `{0} hole{1}` → ko `{0}홀`).
- Keep emoji, ✓ ✗ → · — symbols, numbers, +/- signs, and HTML entities as they are.
- Keep leading/trailing punctuation shape (a fragment that starts with "· " or ends with " —" keeps that).
- Fragments: many strings are pieces of a longer sentence ("This league is", "easier than"). Translate the piece so it still reads in the most likely sentence; do not add words that would duplicate the missing part.
- Proper nouns (course names, people, cities) — return unchanged.
- Code-looking strings (keys, CSS, URLs, IDs) — return unchanged.
- Length: aim for no more than ~1.3× the English length in es; CJK is naturally shorter. Buttons: shortest natural wording.
- Mobile UI casing: es sentence case (not Title Case).
