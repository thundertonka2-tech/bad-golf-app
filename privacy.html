Privacy Policy

Effective: May 23, 2026 · Last updated: May 23, 2026

Bad Golf LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Bad Golf mobile application and web app (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We've tried to write this in plain English. If anything is unclear, contact us at support@badgolf.com.

Summary — the short version

  • We collect very little.
  • We don't sell data. We don't show ads. We don't have analytics SDKs.
  • We don't ask for your email, phone, address, or password.
  • We don't track your location or use GPS.
  • Round data is shared via anonymous round codes — anyone with the code can read and edit that round.
  • Personal data like your player roster is stored on your device.

1. What this Service does

Bad Golf is a golf scoring and side-bet calculator. You enter golf scores, the app calculates who owes who based on the betting games you've chosen, and you settle up with your friends outside the app (Venmo, cash, etc.). Bad Golf does not process payments. No real money moves through the app.

2. Information we collect

Information you enter

  • Player names and handicaps — typed by you during round setup. Can be real names, nicknames, or initials. We have no way to verify these are real.
  • Hole-by-hole scores — entered by you on the Score tab during a round.
  • Course information — par, slope, rating, stroke index, tee names — entered by you when you add a course to the library.
  • Tournament definitions — saved setups (course, players, games, rules) that you can re-launch later.
  • Round codes — automatically generated when you start a round. These are short 6-character codes (e.g. "PINE42") that buddies use to join the same round.

Information we do NOT collect

  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Physical addresses
  • Passwords (the Service has no user accounts at this time)
  • Location or GPS data
  • Payment information (no real money flows through Bad Golf)
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics or behavioral tracking
  • Photos, contacts, or any other phone data

3. How we use the information

The information you enter is used solely to operate the Service:

  • To calculate scores, handicaps, and bet outcomes
  • To share round data with people who have the round code
  • To remember your player roster for future rounds
  • To save tournaments you've set up

We do not use your information for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any commercial purpose beyond providing the Service.

4. Where the information lives

Shared data (in the cloud)

Round data, the shared course library, and tournament definitions are stored on our backend at Supabase (supabase.com), a service that hosts databases and provides real-time sync. This lets all the players in a round see live scoring on their own phones.

Round data is keyed by an anonymous round code. There are no user accounts. Anyone who knows your round code can read and modify that round's data. Only share round codes with people you trust to be in your game.

Personal data (on your device)

Your player roster (the list of names + handicaps you've used in past rounds), your saved tournaments list, and your "me" name designation are stored in your device's local browser storage. They are not uploaded to our servers.

We do not retain login records, IP address logs, or activity history about you as an individual, because we do not operate user accounts.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with marketers or advertisers.

We share data only with:

  • Supabase, our backend hosting provider (described above). Supabase acts as a data processor on our behalf.
  • Legal authorities, if required by valid legal process (subpoena, court order). We would object to overbroad requests where appropriate.

6. Children's privacy

Bad Golf is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and provided information, contact us at support@badgolf.com and we'll remove it.

7. Your choices and rights

Because we collect so little information and don't operate accounts, most data-rights options that apply to bigger apps don't apply here. That said:

  • Access: to see what you've entered, open the app — your data is visible to you in the Rounds, Setup, and Board tabs.
  • Deletion: clear your local data via the in-app Clear function or by uninstalling the app. For shared backend data, email us at support@badgolf.com with your round code.
  • Correction: you can edit player names, scores, course information, and notes directly in the app at any time.
  • Opt-out of data collection: stop using the app, or use it in a private/incognito browser session that doesn't persist storage.

We do not respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals because we do not track you in the first place.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and secure infrastructure at Supabase. However, no internet-based service is 100% secure. Because shared rounds use anonymous round codes, anyone who learns or guesses your round code could read and modify that round's data. Use round codes only with people you trust to be in your game.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the Service after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
